Mind the Top

A physics merge puzzle for Android, from Whizkid World LLP.

A physics merge puzzle: match two, make one bigger, and mind the top.

The game

  • Drop and merge. Two identical pieces that touch become the next one up a chain of eleven, and every drop rolls and nudges the pile.
  • Mind the top. If the pile stays above the line marked TOP, the game is over. Pull it back down in time and you play on.
  • A daily puzzle: the same pieces in the same order for everyone, and one attempt. Past days stay playable from the calendar, and enough days in a month earn a trophy.
  • Seven themes, with the same physics in each; only the look changes. Orchard is free, Sports Day and Crazy Mix are earned by play, and Deep Space, Sweet Shop, In Bloom and Precious Outback can be bought.
  • Boosts, undo and a continue when a run needs help, with a free first use of each boost and of undo.
  • Optional Google Play Games for achievements, two leaderboards and saved progress. Everything else works without it.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads. A one-time purchase, Remove ads, removes the full-screen ads between games; the optional videos for continuing, undoing, boosts and unlocking a theme early stay, if you want them.
