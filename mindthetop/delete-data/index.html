Delete your data — Mind the Top

Mind the Top · Whizkid World LLP

The game has no accounts of its own and keeps your progress on your phone, so you can delete it yourself, in the game, at any time.

In the game

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen), then Privacy › Delete all game data. This erases everything the game has stored on your phone — scores, Daily results, trophies, streak, unlocked themes, settings and any game in progress — and closes the game so it starts fresh. It also resets the identifier that analytics uses, so nothing afterwards is connected to what came before.

It keeps the record of what you have bought, because a purchase belongs to your Google Play account, not to this phone. Uninstalling the game removes everything it has stored on your phone. If you also want to remove the copy Android may have placed in your Google Drive backup, delete the app’s backup from Google Drive › Settings › Backups on your device.

What the game cannot delete for you

  • Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days and then deletes them. A report carries nothing that identifies you, so we have no way to find yours among others and delete it sooner.
  • Analytics. Google Analytics for Firebase keeps the short note of each game played for 14 months and then deletes it. It is tied to an app-instance identifier, not to you; Delete all game data resets that identifier, which is the most that can be done from the phone.
  • Purchases. Google Play holds the record of what you bought, under your Google account, not us. Refunds go through Google Play — see Purchases and refunds.
  • The advertising ID. Android controls it, not the game. Reset or delete it in your phone’s Settings › Privacy › Ads.
