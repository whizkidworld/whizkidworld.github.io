Mind the Top: press kit

A physics merge puzzle for Android, from Whizkid World LLP.

A physics merge puzzle: drop, merge, and mind the TOP line.

Mind the Top is a physics merge puzzle with one simple rule. Drag to aim and let go to drop a piece. When two identical pieces touch, they merge into the next, bigger piece, up a chain of eleven sizes. Pieces roll and push each other, so every drop changes the pile. Near the top of the container is a line marked TOP: if the pile stays above it, the game ends. There are seven themes with the same physics, a daily puzzle that is the same for everyone, and twenty-five achievements.
