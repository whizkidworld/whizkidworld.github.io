Sudoku: In Your Element

A sudoku game for Android, from Whizkid World LLP.

Daily sudoku, hints that teach, six real difficulties. Find your element.

The game

  • Six difficulties, from Beginner to Insane, graded by the solving techniques a puzzle needs, not only by how many numbers are given.
  • Hints that teach. A hint first tells you what to look for, so you learn the technique; it fills the cell only if you ask.
  • A daily puzzle, the same for everyone.
  • Pencil notes, undo and auto-save, and colour themes in light and dark.
  • Optional Google Play Games for cloud save, leaderboards and achievements. Everything else works without it.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads. A one-time purchase, Remove ads, removes the full-screen ads between games; the optional videos for extra hints and second chances stay, if you want them.
