Delete your data

Sudoku: In Your Element · Whiz Kid World LLP

The game has no accounts of its own and keeps your progress on your phone, so you can delete it yourself, in the app, at any time.

In the app

Open Me → Privacy → Delete all app data. This removes scores, medals, daily-challenge history, your profile, every preference and any game in progress, and returns the app to how it was when first installed. Uninstalling the app does the same.

What the app cannot delete for you

  • Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days and then deletes them. A report carries nothing that identifies you, so we have no way to find yours among others and delete it sooner.
  • Google Play Games. If you have used it, its cloud save, leaderboard entries and achievements are held by Google under your account, not by us. Delete them from Play Games' own settings or your Google Account. Deleting the app's data on your phone does not delete them.
  • The advertising ID. Android controls it, not the app. Reset or delete it in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
