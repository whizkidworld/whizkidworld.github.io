Sudoku: In Your Element, press kit

A sudoku game for Android, from Whizkid World LLP.

A calm sudoku with hints that teach and six real difficulty levels.

Sudoku: In Your Element is a calm, clean sudoku that helps you become a better solver. Each puzzle is graded by the solving techniques it needs, not by how many numbers are given. When you are stuck, a hint first names the technique and shows you where to look, so you can learn it. Everyone gets the same daily puzzle, and each month you earn a bronze, silver or gold trophy, depending on how many daily puzzles you solve.
