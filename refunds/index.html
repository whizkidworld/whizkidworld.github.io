Purchases and refunds

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Whizkid World LLP

Our games are free to download. Some offer optional one-time purchases, such as removing the ads between games or unlocking a theme. This page explains how those purchases work and how to get your money back if something is wrong.

How you pay

Every purchase is made through Google Play, using the payment method on your Google account. Google processes the payment and sends the receipt. We never see or store your card or bank details.

Prices are shown in your local currency in the game before you buy, and include any taxes Google applies in your country.

What each game sells

  • Sudoku: In Your Element: Remove ads.
  • Mind the Top: Remove ads; four themes (Deep Space, Sweet Shop, In Bloom, Precious Outback), one at a time or as a pack of all four.
  • Amazing Arrows: Tap Puzzle: Remove ads.

Remove ads removes the full-screen ads between games or levels. Videos you choose to watch for a reward stay available. Each game shows what it sells, and the price, before you buy.

Delivery

Everything sold is digital and delivered in the game, on the Google account that bought it, as soon as Google Play confirms the payment: usually at once. Nothing is shipped.

What you get

  • Each purchase is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Nothing renews or charges you again.
  • A purchase belongs to the Google account that made it. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, sign in to Google Play with the same account and use the game's restore button (Restore purchases; in Sudoku, Restore purchase) to get it back.
  • Purchases have no cash value and cannot be transferred to another account.

Cancellation

A purchase cannot be cancelled once Google Play has completed it, because it is delivered at that moment. A payment still pending can be cancelled from your Google Play order history. To get your money back for a completed purchase, see Refunds below.

Refunds

Within 48 hours of buying, you can usually request a refund directly from Google Play, without contacting us: open Google Play's order history (play.google.com → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history), find the order and choose Request a refund. Google decides these requests under its own refund policy.

After 48 hours, or if Google Play cannot help, write to us at whizkidworldllp@gmail.com with the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and what went wrong. We will look into it; any refund is made through Google Play.

A refunded purchase no longer belongs to your account, so the game removes what it unlocked the next time it opens and Google Play confirms the refund: for example, the ads between games return once Remove ads has been refunded. This needs an internet connection; offline, nothing changes until the game can check with Google Play.

If a purchase does not appear

Payments sometimes take a few minutes to complete, and some payment methods mark a purchase as pending until they confirm it. The game unlocks it as soon as Google Play confirms the payment. If it still has not appeared after a day, use the game's restore button (Restore purchases; in Sudoku, Restore purchase), then write to us with the order number.
