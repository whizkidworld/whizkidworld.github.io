Amazing Arrows

A tap puzzle for Android, from Whizkid World LLP.

Clear the board, one arrow at a time.

The game

  • Tap an arrow and it slides off the board the way it points, unless another arrow is in its path. Find the order that clears them all.
  • Seven modes, each adding one twist: arrows that stay locked until their key has left, mirrors that bend a path, portals that send it elsewhere, linked arrows that leave together, and countdowns to beat, then all of them at once.
  • Pictures to collect. Many boards are shaped like something, and clearing one reveals its picture for your picture book.
  • A daily puzzle and a weekly challenge, the same board for everyone.
  • Scores for clearing quickly and cleanly, with a best for every level, and three strike settings, from relaxed to hardcore.
  • Optional Google Play Games for achievements, leaderboards and saved progress. Everything else works without it.

Free, with ads

The game is free and shows ads. A one-time purchase, Remove ads, removes the full-screen ads between levels; the optional videos for an extra strike or an extra hint stay, if you want them.
