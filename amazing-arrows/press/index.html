Amazing Arrows: press kit

A tap puzzle for Android, from Whizkid World LLP.

Tap arrows off the board to reveal pictures.

Every arrow wants to leave the board. Tap an arrow and it slides off along its own bent path, like a train on a track. If something is in the way, it bounces back and you get a strike. Find the right order and clear the whole board. Many boards hide a picture: a cat, a rocket, a crown, a whale. Clear the board and the picture fills in with colour, for a picture book of 38. Seven modes add locks, mirrors, portals, linked arrows and countdowns, then all of them at once, and a daily puzzle and a weekly challenge give everyone the same board.
