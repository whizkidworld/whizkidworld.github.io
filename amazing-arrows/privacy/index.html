Privacy Policy — Amazing Arrows: Tap Puzzle

Effective 16 October 2026.

Amazing Arrows: Tap Puzzle (com.whizkidworld.amazingarrows) is a puzzle game published by Whizkid World LLP (“we”, “us”), which is responsible for the information described here.

The short version: the app sends nothing to us. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Every board is generated on your device. The only thing that can leave your phone is what Google Play Games keeps for you, and only if you use it (see below).

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device. It goes nowhere else, except the copy Google Play Games saves to your own Google account if you use it:

  • Level progress, stars and best times, in every mode
  • The daily puzzle and weekly challenge results, and your daily streak
  • Badges earned, the picture book, and play statistics (such as your best combo)
  • A level in progress, so you can pick it up later
  • Settings: sound, vibration, theme, colours, strike setting, and the daily reminder’s time

You can erase all of this at any time from Settings → Erase all progress (which removes everything listed here, settings included), by clearing the app’s data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Google Play Games

Google Play Games is optional; everything in the app works without it. If you have a Play Games profile and have allowed it, Play Games signs you in automatically when the app starts; you can turn that off in the Google Play Games app, or sign in later from the app. While you are signed in, the app uses Play Games for three things:

  • Weekly leaderboard. Your best time on the weekly challenge is posted to a Play Games leaderboard. Other players can see it with your Play Games name and picture, according to your Play Games profile settings; a private profile keeps them hidden.
  • Achievements. When you earn a badge in the app, it is also unlocked in Play Games, which shows it on your Play Games profile.
  • Cloud save. A copy of your progress, badges and daily streak is saved to your own Google account in Play Games’ saved games, so it can be restored on another phone.

All of this is kept by Google under your Google account and Google’s terms. The app has no server of its own, so none of it comes to us. To delete it, open the Google Play Games app and use its settings to delete your Play Games data for this game, or your whole Play Games profile.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder (the app asks once, after your first daily puzzle, and you can change it in Settings), the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. It is created and shown by your phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Turning the reminder off cancels it.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children: we receive nothing. Google Play Games, if used, is Google’s service under the player’s own Google account.

Future versions

If a future version adds anything that sends information off your device (for example advertising or crash reports), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and will say exactly what is sent, to whom and why.
